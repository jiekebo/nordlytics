Shot Log

Automatic target scoring for shooting sports. Clip a camera to the target rig, take a photo of the paper, and computer vision does the rest: the image is rectified to a flat, metric view, every bullet impact is detected with instance segmentation, and each shot is projected onto the scoring rings for an automatic value — no manual entry.

Around the model sits a full product: clubs, members, events, series and long-term statistics, with a workflow that supports club administration and witness-approved series.
