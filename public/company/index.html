About

Nordlytics AB.

An independent Swedish technology consultancy, founded in 2021 and headquartered in Malmö — helping companies design, build and operate AI-powered products and data platforms.

Our focus

Nordlytics specialises in applied artificial intelligence: machine-learning systems, computer vision, time-series analytics and the cloud platforms that carry them to production. We work with enterprises, utilities and product companies that need senior engineering ownership rather than slideware.

We deliberately stay small and senior. Every engagement is led hands-on by our founder, drawing on more than a decade of machine-learning engineering built at companies including Apple, E.ON, Sony Mobile and Twilio — from automated ML platforms and grid forecasting to large-scale behavioural analytics.

Our own products — such as Shot Log, an automatic computer-vision scoring system — prove the same approach we bring to client work: custom-curated datasets, honest evaluation, production engineering and systems that keep running long after launch.

Jacob Stig Salomonsen

Founder, CEO & Board member

MSc in Computer Science from the University of Copenhagen. Over fifteen years of software engineering — the last decade focused on machine learning, computer vision and cloud data platforms.
