Products

Built in-house, running in production.

Shot Log — automatic target scoring

Our flagship product brings computer vision to shooting sports: point a camera at a paper target and Shot Log rectifies the image, detects every bullet impact and scores it automatically — no manual entry. Clubs, events, series and statistics included.

Computer Vision ONNX iOS · Android · Web FastAPI
View our products

How it works

  • Capture the target with a phone camera
  • Homography rectifies the paper to a flat view
  • An instance-segmentation model finds every impact
  • Impacts are projected onto scoring rings automatically

About

Independent. Senior. Nordic.

Nordlytics AB is an independent Swedish technology consultancy, founded in 2021 and headquartered in Malmö. We work with enterprises and utilities that need intelligent software built properly — from first prototype to systems that run autonomously in production.

Our founder brings over a decade of machine-learning engineering from companies like Apple, E.ON, Sony Mobile and Twilio, and that standard of ownership carries into every engagement we take on.
